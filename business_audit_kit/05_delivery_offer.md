# Delivery Offer: Claude Business Starter Setup

Use this as your service description, proposal section, or sales page copy.

---

## What's Included

**Claude Business Starter Setup** gives your team a production-ready Claude practice in one engagement.

| Deliverable | Description |
|---|---|
| **Business Audit** | Intake interview + workflow discovery session to identify your top AI opportunities |
| **5 Custom Workflows** | Fully built prompt templates tuned to your business, brand voice, and data |
| **Reusable Prompt Library** | All workflows saved in a structured library your team can copy, run, and adapt |
| **Team Cheat Sheet** | One-page reference guide — how to use Claude, when to use it, what to never skip |
| **1 Training Session** | Live walkthrough with your team so everyone can run workflows confidently on day one |

---

## How It Works

1. **Intake call** (60 min) — Complete the Client Intake Form and walk through the Workflow Discovery Sheet together
2. **Audit delivery** (async) — You receive a prioritized list of your top 10 workflow opportunities with effort/impact scores
3. **Build sprint** (3–5 business days) — We build your 5 highest-value workflows using the Claude Workflow Template
4. **Review & handoff** (60 min) — We walk through every workflow, answer questions, and hand off the prompt library
5. **Training session** (90 min) — Live session with your team covering usage, best practices, and what to flag for human review
