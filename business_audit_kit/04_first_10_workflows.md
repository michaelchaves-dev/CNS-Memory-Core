# First 10 Workflows

Pre-built workflow definitions for the most common business tasks. Use these as starting points — customize the ROLE, RULES, and OUTPUT sections for each client.

---

## 1. Customer Email Reply

**Role:** Customer success rep / support agent  
**Input:** Incoming customer email + relevant account context  
**Task:** Draft a clear, on-brand reply that addresses every point raised  
**Rules:** Match client tone; do not promise refunds or SLA changes without flag  
**Output:** Ready-to-send email draft (subject line + body)

---

## 2. Proposal Draft

**Role:** Sales writer  
**Input:** Client intake notes, budget range, service scope  
**Task:** Draft a professional services proposal with executive summary, scope, timeline, and pricing section placeholders  
**Rules:** No invented pricing; flag any scope gaps for owner to fill  
**Output:** Structured proposal document (markdown or Word-ready)

---

## 3. Social Post

**Role:** Social media writer  
**Input:** Topic, platform (LinkedIn / Instagram / X), any key message or link  
**Task:** Write 3 post variations with hooks, body, and CTA  
**Rules:** Match brand voice; include relevant hashtags; no competitor mentions  
**Output:** 3 labeled drafts with character counts

---

## 4. SOP Creation

**Role:** Operations writer  
**Input:** Process description (voice note transcript, bullet notes, or walkthrough recording summary)  
**Task:** Convert raw notes into a numbered SOP with steps, responsible roles, and decision points  
**Rules:** Use plain language; flag any step where the process is ambiguous  
**Output:** Formatted SOP (title, purpose, steps, roles, exceptions)

---

## 5. Meeting Summary

**Role:** Executive assistant  
**Input:** Meeting transcript or raw notes  
**Task:** Extract decisions made, action items (with owners and deadlines), open questions, and next meeting date  
**Rules:** Do not paraphrase — quote direct commitments accurately  
**Output:** Structured summary (Decisions / Action Items / Open Items / Next Steps)

---

## 6. Vendor Comparison

**Role:** Procurement analyst  
**Input:** Vendor names, feature criteria, any pricing or spec sheets provided  
**Task:** Build a comparison table and write a recommendation paragraph  
**Rules:** Only use information provided; flag missing data per vendor  
**Output:** Comparison table + 2–3 sentence recommendation with reasoning

---

## 7. Job Posting

**Role:** HR / Recruiting writer  
**Input:** Role title, team, key responsibilities, required skills, compensation range (optional)  
**Task:** Write a compelling job posting that attracts qualified candidates  
**Rules:** No discriminatory language; follow client brand voice; flag if comp range is omitted  
**Output:** Full job posting (title, about the company, responsibilities, requirements, how to apply)

---

## 8. Complaint Response

**Role:** Customer experience manager  
**Input:** Customer complaint (email or form submission) + relevant context  
**Task:** Draft an empathetic, solution-oriented response that de-escalates and offers a clear next step  
**Rules:** Acknowledge the issue before defending policy; do not offer compensation without flag  
**Output:** Response draft + internal note flagging any policy/escalation concerns

---

## 9. Weekly Report

**Role:** Operations analyst  
**Input:** Raw metrics or update bullets from each team  
**Task:** Synthesize into a weekly summary with highlights, lowlights, and key focus for next week  
**Rules:** Use numbers where provided; flag any metric that is missing or unclear  
**Output:** Executive-ready report (3–5 paragraphs or structured sections)

---

## 10. FAQ Builder

**Role:** Content writer  
**Input:** Product/service documentation, common support tickets, or interview notes  
**Task:** Extract the top questions and write clear, concise answers for each  
**Rules:** Keep answers under 75 words each; flag any question where the answer is unclear or missing  
**Output:** Numbered FAQ list (Q: / A: format), ready to publish or import
